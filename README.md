# Ice Age: Dawn of the Dinosaurs

## My Favorite Movie

**Ice Age: Dawn of the Dinosaurs** is the *third movie* in the Ice Age series. It was released in **2009** and directed by Carlos Saldanha.

---

## Story Summary

> A story about friendship, family, and surviving a wild adventure.

Sid finds three giant eggs and takes care of them. When the eggs hatch, a dinosaur mother takes the babies and Sid with her to a hidden underground world. Manny, Diego, and the herd go on a dangerous trip to rescue him.

---

## Main Characters

- Manny the mammoth
- Sid the sloth
- Diego the saber-toothed tiger
- Ellie the mammoth
- Crash and Eddie the possums
- Buck the weasel

## Why I Love This Movie

1. It is funny.
2. It has exciting adventures.
3. It shows how important family and friends are.
